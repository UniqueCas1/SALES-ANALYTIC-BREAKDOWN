# Exploring business performance of the e-commerce company: Descriptive review in four year
### OVERALL BUSINESS ANALYSIS - DESCRIPTIVE ANALYSIS

#### OVERVIEW
This analysis provided insights into the general performance of an e-commerce enterprice in three fiscal year (2003-2005), revealing seasonal trends all year round and identifying revenue patterns, by analyzing various metrics of the business: Total sales, total profit, sales YoY, Profit YoY, Profit margin, Sales over time by month, orders by deal size, etc.

### PERFORMANCE HIGHLIGHTS  

- Total Sales: $10.03 million — remarkably up 21.7% from last year.

- Total Profit: $35.29K — shows a staggering 94.6% increase in the most recent year compared to the previous, indicating improved cost efficiency.

- Overall Profit Margin: 0.35% — still relatively low, suggesting room for better pricing or operational optimisation, cost control, and high-margin product prioritisation.

- Customers & Sales Trends
92 active customers, with the top 5 (led by Diego Freyre and Valerie Nelson) driving most of the revenue. I suggest the need to diversify the customer base.

Strong sales peaks are seen between October and November, implying seasonal demand.


- Regional & Product Insights
North America led in sales, followed by Europe (EMEA) and Asia (APAC).

Classic Cars ranked as the best-selling product line, with Vintage Cars and Motorcycles following closely.
